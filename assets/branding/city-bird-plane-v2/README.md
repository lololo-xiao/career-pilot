# CareerPilot city, bird, and paper-plane concept (V2)

This alternate direction removes the multicolor metaphor from V1. The person is represented
by a living bird: self-directed, capable, and already in flight. Pilot is represented by the
paper airplane: an intentional companion traveling beside the person rather than replacing
or pulling them. Tall buildings place that relationship in a quiet evening city.

The imagery is an original interpretation of the user's requested themes. It does not quote
lyrics or copy song, album, or promotional artwork.

## Visual system

- Deep ink: `#182421`
- Warm paper: `#F4F0E8`
- Thumbnail atmosphere only: muted dusk blue-gray `#687378`
- App icon and launch screen: strict two-color treatment

The city belongs in large-format storytelling artwork. At app-icon scale, it is removed so
the bird and paper airplane remain legible and emotionally clear.

## Deliverables

- `careerpilot-thumbnail-city-companion-v2-1536x1024.png`: project thumbnail, exact 3:2,
  opaque sRGB PNG, under 5 MB.
- `careerpilot-app-icon-bird-plane-v2-1024.png`: iOS App Store icon master, 1024 square,

These files are review-ready alternatives and do not overwrite the active Xcode assets.

## Generation method and final prompts

The source artwork was created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The icon and
launch artwork were then flattened locally to the exact two-color palette, resized, and
normalized to opaque sRGB production PNGs.

### Thumbnail

> Create an original, refined, near-monochrome 3:2 editorial poster. Show exactly one living
> bird representing the person and exactly one folded-paper airplane representing the AI
> agent, flying close together in the same direction above a quiet city of tall, slender
> buildings. Keep most of the composition as open late-dusk sky. Use deep ink charcoal,
> warm paper ivory, and muted dusk blue-gray only, with precise negative space and subtle
> matte grain. No text, lyrics, logos, people, compass, saturated accents, identifiable
> landmarks, album-art imitation, border, or watermark.

### App icon

> Create an extremely minimal 1:1 iOS icon with exactly one smooth, original soaring-bird
> silhouette and one smaller folded-paper-airplane silhouette beside it, aligned upward as
> companions. Use only a deep-ink full-bleed background and warm-paper mark. Keep the shapes
> compact, crisp, centered, and readable at 32 px, with generous room for Apple's icon mask.
> No skyline, trails, text, gradient, texture, shadow, compass, circle, rounded corners,
> border, or watermark.

### Launch artwork

> Recompose the same bird-and-paper-plane relationship as a quiet square launch screen.
> Invert the icon colors: deep-ink paired mark on a full-bleed warm-paper background. Center
> the mark at no more than 25% of the canvas and keep all meaningful artwork inside the
> central 34% for portrait and landscape aspect-fill crops. No other objects, color,
> gradient, texture, text, border, rounded corners, or watermark.
